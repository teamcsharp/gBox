## How the numbers were obtained
- Source: `gbox-data/mercs/lessons/<title>/runs/*.jsonl`; each line's `lessons[]` entries (`id`, `outcome`, `observed{}`).
- Parser: plain `json` (python). 18,380 raw entries, because `round_recall` lines repeat lessons across files; deduped by `(title, id)` to 1,652 unique.
- `outcomes`: the recorded `outcome` field. `per_stage_died_total`: `[died, all unique lessons]` by `observed.stage` ("unknown" = field absent).
