# Character fitting and experimental texture review

Open **Fit & textures…** from a game's extracted model, or **Retarget** from a cast-plan row. The left view keeps the original donor visible; the right view shows the adjusted fit over the target reference. Streamed Liberty City Stories pedestrians use their exact native model when the base ISO is already staged. Other targets can use a collected reference; the editor labels a fallback rather than promising an exact target.

Use **Fit & proportions** to adjust height, ground lift, head size, torso width, arm/leg length or an individual body part. **Shape cage** supplies a 3×3×3 quadratic lattice before retargeting. Choose Back/Middle/Front to expose nine circles; drag a circle in the view, or Alt-drag to change depth. Offsets are bounded to a quarter of donor height. Mesh topology, UVs, skin weights and rig anchors stay intact; the writer transforms normals through the deformation Jacobian and rejects folded/collapsed cages. This is lattice deformation, not a geodesic voxel binding implementation.

**Plant mesh feet** aligns the lowest vertices assigned to the foot roles with the reference ground, plus the chosen ground lift. This is a rest-pose aid, not animated foot IK or a guarantee against weapon/body collisions. Check walking, crouching and attack poses in the game. Save per slot or for the whole game; **Save and rebuild this slot** creates a build without launching it. GTA III, Vice City and Liberty City Stories now pass the saved profile into their native fits.

Experimental texture processing is off by default. Enable it, select color maps, choose 1K/2K/4K (longest edge, aspect preserved), and select Conservative, Smooth transitions or Detail/sharpening. Local AI uses the configured ComfyUI service (`texture_comfy_url`) and installed `RealESRGAN_x4plus.safetensors`; **Resize only · no AI** is a separate, explicitly labeled choice. Missing/unavailable AI never silently becomes resizing. A timed-out pending prompt is removed only from that review's queue entry; other work is untouched.

The before/after images require **Accept and use enhanced donor** or **Reject · keep original**. A pending decision blocks rebuilding through the editor and survives reloading that editor. Accept creates a separate donor with the approved maps and carries unsaved fitting adjustments into it; reject imports nothing. Original alpha is preserved separately, and normal/roughness/data references are unchanged even if they originally shared a color texture. Original models, geometry and maps are preserved. Approved variants remain unverified in-game; subsequent cast plans use the latest explicitly approved texture donor for that character.

These are enhanced source maps. A target writer can downsample/repack them into its native texture allocations. In particular, the PS2 Liberty City Stories writer retains the original small texture budgets: this feature does **not** implement a 4K emulator replacement-texture pack or enlarge the game's GPU texture allocations.

For an LCS pedestrian cast, use source **Dead or Alive universe**, mapping **Random**, and slots **Pedestrians**. The writer covers 93 streamed pedestrian models, with each model's own 24-bone skeleton and texture allocation. Randomness is seeded per model slot; all spawns using one slot share its assigned donor, rather than using a new random character on every spawn. Player outfits and separate story/cutscene models are distinct. Prerecorded movies are unchanged.

Every requested pedestrian must encode successfully before the base ISO is copied once and patched into a new build. Written chunks are read back. Changed plans/profiles and explicit rebuilds get distinct names. Oversized or conflicting allocations fail without publishing a partially replaced ROM; failed disc writes remove only the new partial staging image, retaining a small diagnostic. Encoding/readback are not gameplay verification. Severe native decimation can lose detail that texture enhancement cannot recover.
